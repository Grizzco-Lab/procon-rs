ProCon Studio: icons and art

Stickers on a 24×24 grid in the spirit of Salmon Run: flat fills from the palette below, a thick dark outline and chunky, goofy shapes. Use one with <svg class="app-icon"><use href="/icons/app-studio.svg#i"/></svg>. Each icon carries its own colours; a theme tints them with --icon-line (outlines and pupils), --icon-accent (the one ink accent) and --icon-halo (a sticker edge around the whole icon, none by default).

Palette

The Salmon Run theme (art/salmon.css, 4th entry in the View menu). Body text on the cards: paper #f6f1e4 14.8:1, #cfc8b4 10:1, #a39d8a 6.1:1; dark ink on every band colour at least 5.3:1 (WCAG AA).

Artwork

In web/art/, served at /art/. The theme uses the sign, Vision and Predictor pieces as empty states, the magnifier and the old reviewer as corner characters on the Inkspector's and Cuttlefish's lists, and the tiles behind the page and inside the cards.
